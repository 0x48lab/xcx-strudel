0Get ready: add the extension

The project of each step already has the extension, so you do not need this when you start from a step.

To make your own project from scratch, this button is the easiest. It opens a new project with the extension.

New project

To add the extension to a project you are working on, load it like this.

  1. Open the Xcratch editor.
  2. Click the "Add Extension" button at the bottom left.
  3. Choose "Extension Loader", the first card.
  4. Enter this URL and click "OK". https://0x48lab.github.io/xcx-strudel/dist/strudel.mjs

When "Strudel" appears on the left, you are ready. The green blocks with the swirl are the blocks of this extension.

At first only the "beginner" blocks are shown. Choose "normal" or "master" in the top block to show more blocks.

If you hear nothing, click the green flag again. Browsers do not play sound until you touch the page.

1Play a sound

"play [ ] on track [ ]" is the most important block. Click the green flag, and the bass drum plays "boom, boom..." again and again. Click the red button to stop.

Click ▼ to choose another sound: drums, the notes C to high C, and "rest".

Try it
  • Change it to snare or clap. You can change it while it plays.
  • What does "C" sound like?
Open this step

2Sounds in order

Put the "in order" block into the ▼ of "play", and four sounds play one after another: "boom, -, tap, -". That is a beat.

What is a cycle?

In Strudel a loop is called a "cycle". The sounds of "in order" fit exactly into one cycle: four sounds share it in four, eight sounds in eight. Use "set tempo to [ ] BPM" to change the speed.

You can name the track (here "drums") as you like.

Try it
  • Change a "rest" to hi-hat. What happens?
  • Set the tempo to 80 or 140.
Open this step

3Layer tracks

Add another "play" block with another track name, and the two play together, like instruments joining a band.

"repeat [ ] [ ] times" plays the same sound many times in a cycle. Eight hi-hats make the beat groove.

Playing again on the same track name replaces the old pattern. You will use this in step 5.

Try it
  • Repeat 16 or 3 times.
  • Use "together" to play bass drum and crash at the same time.
Open this step

4Make a melody

A third track "melody" is added. The blocks are nested, so read them from the inside.

  1. "in order C E G high C" and "in order A G E D" make two phrases.
  2. "take turns" plays them one after another, a phrase in each cycle.
  3. "[ ] with tone [ ]" chooses the kind of sound: triangle, sawtooth, square, sine and more.
Try it
  • Change the tone to sawtooth or square.
  • Change the notes and make your own melody.
Open this step

5Change the song with keys

Changing the music while it plays is the most fun part of Strudel. It is called live coding.

  • Space: plays another rhythm on the "drums" track. The track name is the same, so it replaces the old rhythm.
  • s: "stop track [ ]" stops only the hats.
  • 0: "stop all tracks".

The change happens at the start of the next cycle, so the rhythm never slips, whenever you press a key.

Click the stage once before you use keys.

Try it
  • Add keys so that you can switch between three rhythms.
Open this step

"show [ ]" draws the sound on the stage.

In the piano roll, the vertical line in the middle is "now". Higher notes are drawn higher. The picture is drawn behind the sprites, so you can still see the cat.

7Make the cat dance

These blocks move sprites with the music.

  • "when a beat comes": runs on every beat. With "next costume" the cat steps to the beat.
  • "when [ ] sounds": runs each time the chosen drum plays. Jump on the bass drum, change the color on the snare...

They work with all the usual Scratch blocks.

Try it
  • When the snare sounds, "change color effect by 25".
  • Use the "beat" and "cycle" values to move the cat.

8Normal level: mini-notation, effects and scales

Choose "normal" in the top block to show more blocks.

Mini-notation

"sounds in mini-notation" writes sounds just like Strudel. The drums here are bd*2 [~ sd] <hh oh> sd and the bass is <c2 a1 f1 g1>*2 (long texts are cut with "…" in the block).

WriteMeaningExample
bd sdsounds separated by spaces play in orderbd hh sd hh
~restbd ~ sd ~
*2play it twicehh*8
[ ]fit into the time of onebd [sd sd]
< >one in each cycle, taking turns<bd cp>
,play at the same timebd*4, hh*8

Effects and scales

  • "[ ] with effect [ ] [ ]": muffle (lpf), echo in a room (room), delay and more. The bass is muffled at 600 to sound soft.
  • "steps [ ] of scale [ ] [ ]": write notes as step numbers of a scale. On the major pentatonic scale, any number sounds nice. The "piano" tone loads real piano sounds automatically.

9Write a song with lists and rhythms

Long chord progressions and melodies are hard to read in one line of mini-notation. Write them in Scratch lists, a bar in each item, and join them with a block. The rest of the tutorial writes songs with lists.

Open this step

1. Make a list

Click "Make a List" in "Variables" and give it a name. This project already has five lists: "chords", "bass", "melody", "rhythm" and "drums".

Check the box next to a list to show it on the stage. Click an item to change it, and "+" to add one.

Write one bar of mini-notation in each item. A chord is written like [f3,a3,c4]: the notes to play together, separated by , and put in [ ].

These chords, F, G, Em and Am, are a progression which is used very often in pop music.

2. Play a list

Put "items of list [ ] [ ]" into the text of "notes in mini-notation". The items are joined into one mini-notation.

Choose with ▼Mini-notationUse
one in each cycle<[item1] [item2] …>an item = a bar: lists "chords", "bass", "melody"
in order in a cycle[item1] [item2] …an item = a beat: list "rhythm"
all together[item1], [item2], …an item = a drum: list "drums"

Press 1 and the chords ring, a bar each. Before pressing keys, click the stage outside the list (keys typed after clicking in the list change the list).

3. Play on a rhythm

"play [ ] in rhythm [ ]" plays only on x; ~ is a rest. The chords stay the same and get a rhythm.

The rhythm is in a list too. The items of "rhythm" are x ~, ~ x, x x and ~ x. "in order in a cycle" makes each item a beat, each beat cut in two. Key 2 replaces the piano and it starts to bounce.

Beat1234
list "rhythm"x ~~ xx x~ x

Try it: make all four items x for quarter notes, or ~ x for off-beats. The chords are the same, but the feel changes a lot.

In Strudel

note("<[[f3,a3,c4]] [[g3,b3,d4]] [[e3,g3,b3]] [[a3,c4,e4]]>")
  .struct("[x ~] [~ x] [x x] [~ x]").s("piano")
s("[bd [~ bd] ~ ~], [~ ~ sd ~], [hh*8]")

4. Add bass, melody and drums

Bass and melody are in lists too, a bar in each item. Add them with keys 3, 4 and 5. Key 0 stops everything.

The drums are "all together": in the list "drums", each item is a line for one drum, like the screen of a drum machine.

DrumItem of "drums"
bass drumbd [~ bd] ~ ~
snare~ ~ sd ~
hi-hathh*8
Barchordsbassmelody
1[f3,a3,c4]f2 [~ f2] f2 [~ f2][c5 d5] [e5 ~] [d5 c5] [a4 ~]
2[g3,b3,d4]g2 [~ g2] g2 [~ g2][b4 c5] [d5 ~] [g4 ~] ~
3[e3,g3,b3]e2 [~ e2] e2 [~ e2][e5 d5] [b4 ~] [g4 b4] [d5 ~]
4[a3,c4,e4]a2 [~ a2] a2 [~ a2][c5 ~] [b4 a4] a4 ~

Read across: the notes of the same bar are in one row. A melody sounds nice when it uses the notes of the chord of its bar.

Try it
  • Swap the second and third items of "chords".
  • Add ~ cp ~ cp to "drums" for claps.
  • Add four items to "melody" for an eight-bar melody (chords and bass repeat every four bars).

10Make a beat with your voice

A voice you record in Scratch, or a sound which comes with Scratch, can be an instrument of Strudel. A beat made of your own voice is a lot of fun.

Open this step

1. Record your voice

  1. Open the "Sounds" tab at the top.
  2. Hover on "Choose a Sound" at the bottom left, and click the microphone (Record).
  3. If the browser asks to use the microphone, click "Allow".
  4. Click the red "Record" button and speak. Stop, listen with "Play", and click "Save".

Short sounds like "boom", "pah" and "tss" work best. The recording is added to the list of sounds with a name like "recording1". This project has the cat's "Meow" at first.

2. Play the voice

Choose a sound with ▼ of "sprite sound [ ]". Your recordings appear there. This block fits into the ▼ of "play" and "in order", just like a drum.

3. Repeat and shorten

"repeat 4 times" plays it four times in a cycle. If the voice is long and overlaps, cut it with "length (clip)".

4. Change the pitch

The "speed" effect changes the pitch. 1 1.5 2 -1 gives the four voices these pitches in turn.

speedWhat happens?
1the voice as it is
2an octave higher, and faster
0.5an octave lower, and slower
-1backwards

5. Mix it with drums

"sprite sound" also fits into "in order": "boom, meow, tap, meow".

Key 5 changes the pitch every bar and adds reverb. Key 0 stops everything.

Try it
  • Record your voice and choose it in every "sprite sound".
  • Record "boom", "pah" and "tss" and use them instead of bass drum, snare and hi-hat.
  • At the master level, "cut each sound of [ ] into [ ]" chops a long voice into small pieces.

Recordings live only in the browser, so they do not play in strudel.cc. When you save the project, the recordings are saved with it.

11Chords of jazz and soul

An original song with chords of jazz and soul, which you hear in stylish pop (the same song as the sample "Soul piano pop"). Chords, bass and melody are in lists, a bar in each item.

Open this step

The secret of the chords

The progression is | FM7 | Em7 A7 | Dm7 G7 | Cmaj9 C9 | FM7 | Fm6 | Em7 E♭dim7 | Dm7 G7sus4 |. Compare it with the simple progression of step 9 (F, G, Em, Am).

BarTrickListen for
allsevenths and ninths (M7, m7, 9)one or two notes added to plain triads make them stylish
2Em7 → A7A7 has C♯, which is not a white key of C major, so it sounds a little sad
3Dm7 → G7 (two-five)the basic move of jazz; it wants to go home to the next chord
6Fm6the bright F turns into a dark Fm: bittersweet
7Em7 → E♭dim7 → Dm7the bass walks down by half steps: E, E♭, D

The piano leaves out the roots. The bass plays them, so the piano plays only the notes which give the color.

The shuffle

This song cuts a beat into three, so it bounces: a shuffle. As in step 9, the rhythm and the drums are lists.

ListHowItems
rhythmin order in a cycle (an item = a beat)x ~ ~ ~ ~ x x ~ x ~ ~ ~
drumsall together (an item = a drum)[bd ~ ~] [~ ~ bd] [~ ~ bd] [~ ~ ~]
[~ ~ ~] [sd ~ ~] [~ ~ ~] [sd ~ ~]
[hh ~ hh]*4

The bass and melody lists also cut a beat into three.

Try it
  • Change the sixth chord [f3,ab3,c4,d4] (Fm6) to [f3,a3,c4,e4] (FM7) and compare. The bittersweet feeling is gone.
  • Change the list "rhythm" to x ~ ~ x x x ~ x like step 9, and it stops bouncing.

12Master level: acid and trance gate

Choose "master" to use the tricks of club music.

  • "spread [ ] of [ ] over [ ] steps": spreads, say, 5 hits over 8 steps as evenly as possible (a Euclidean rhythm). These rhythms are found in music all over the world.
  • "[ ] on drum machine [ ]": the sounds of famous drum machines such as the TR-909.
  • "wave": put it where a number goes, and the value moves like a wave. Here the hi-hats move left and right.
  • "make [ ] acid": the squelchy bass of the TB-303.
  • "trance gate": chops long chords into short pieces. Change "rhythm" to change the chopping.
Try it
  • Spread 3 or 7 hits.
  • Change "rhythm" of the trance gate from 0 to 20 to find one you like.

13Advanced: sounds of EDM and trance

EDM and trance are made of several "parts". Press the keys one by one to add parts, and listen to what each one does. The tempo is 140 BPM.

KeyPartKeyPart
1kick5lead
2off-beat bass6build-up (snare + riser)
3pad with a trance gateSpacedrop (stop the build-up)
4arpeggio pluck7pumping (1 goes back)

Key 0 stops everything. Each part starts at the beginning of the next cycle.

As in step 9, the music is in lists, a bar in each item.

ListItems (a bar each)Used by
chords[a3,c4,e4,a4] [f3,a3,c4,f4] [c4,e4,g4,c5] [g3,b3,d4,g4]pad and pluck (two parts share one list)
bass[~ a1]*4 [~ f1]*4 [~ c2]*4 [~ g1]*4off-beat bass
lead[7 ~ 9 11] [12 11 ~ 9] and more, 4 barslead (scale steps)
build-upsd*4 sd*8 sd*16 sd*32snare

Change the list "chords", and the pad and the pluck change together.

Open the advanced lesson

1. Kick: four on the floor

Four kicks a bar: "boom, boom, boom, boom". This is the heart of dance music. The TR-909 is a famous drum machine of house and techno.

In Strudel

s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909")

2. Off-beat bass

[~ a1]*4 in the list "bass" is "rest, A" four times. It plays only between the kicks (off-beats), so it bounces with the kick without bumping into it. "muffle 500" cuts the high sounds and keeps the low end.

In Strudel

note("<[~ a1]*4 [~ f1]*4 [~ c2]*4 [~ g1]*4>").s("sawtooth").lpf(500)

Try it: change [~ a1]*4 to [~ a1 a1 a1]*4 for the "rolling bass" of trance.

3. Pad with a trance gate

The chords of the list "chords" are held with "supersaw" (many sawtooth waves together) and chopped by the trance gate. "room" makes it sound like a big hall.

In Strudel

note("<[a3,c4,e4,a4] [f3,a3,c4,f4] [c4,e4,g4,c5] [g3,b3,d4,g4]>").s("supersaw")
  .trancegate(.8, 45, 1).room(.6)

The trance gate is a block of this extension. When you open the code in strudel.cc, it becomes Strudel functions which do the same (struct and fillGaps).

4. Arpeggio pluck

Playing the notes of a chord one by one is an arpeggio. It uses the same list "chords" as the pad. "length .3" makes the notes short like a plucked string, and "delay" repeats them later so they sparkle.

In Strudel

note("<[a3,c4,e4,a4] [f3,a3,c4,f4] [c4,e4,g4,c5] [g3,b3,d4,g4]>")
  .arp("0 1 2 3 2 1 2 3").s("square").clip(.3).delay(.4)

5. Thick lead

Three tricks make the melody stand out.

  • thickness (unison) 7: seven sawtooth waves, each a little off in pitch. Like a choir, many voices sound thick.
  • superimpose transpose 12: the same melody also plays an octave (12 semitones) higher, so it shines. superimpose puts what the function in ▼ makes (transpose 12) on top of the melody.
  • a big room: it spreads far.

In Strudel

n("<[7 ~ 9 11] [12 11 ~ 9] [11 ~ 9 7] [8 ~ 9 6]>*2").scale("A3:minor")
  .s("supersaw").superimpose(x => x.transpose(12)).unison(7).room(.8)

Try it: compare thickness 1 and 7 to hear what "thick" means. Try "detune" and "stereo spread (spread)" too.

6. Build-up and drop

The part which lifts the mood before the chorus is the build-up.

  • snare: the list "build-up" goes sd*4 → sd*8 → sd*16 → sd*32, twice as fast every bar. "wave rising" makes it louder.
  • riser: "noise" is a hiss. Raising "thin (hpf)" from 300 to 9000 cuts the low sounds and makes a rising "whoosh".

At the peak, press Space! The build-up stops and the other parts hit: the drop. The waves go around once in 4 cycles, so press it when they reach the top.

In Strudel

s("<sd*4 sd*8 sd*16 sd*32>").bank("RolandTR909").gain(saw.range(.3, 1).slow(4))
s("white*16").hpf(saw.range(300, 9000).slow(4)).gain(saw.range(0, .4).slow(4))

7. Pumping

The biggest secret of EDM. On each kick, all the other tracks get quieter for a moment and come back, so the whole sound breathes. Producers call it "sidechain".

The track name is the same "kick", so 7 replaces the plain kick. Press 1 and 7 a few times and compare. "depth" goes from 0 (no ducking) to 1 (the most).

In Strudel

s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").orbit(2).duckorbit(1).duckattack(.2).duckdepth(.85)

Strudel sends sounds through "orbits". Normal sounds go through orbit 1. The kick goes through orbit 2 and ducks orbit 1.
