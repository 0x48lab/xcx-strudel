New project

To add it to a project you are working on:

  1. Open the Xcratch editor
  2. Click the "Add Extension" button at the bottom left
  3. Choose "Extension Loader"
  4. Enter this URL and click "OK"
https://0x48lab.github.io/xcx-strudel/dist/strudel.mjs

Shapes of blocks

  • "play on track [ ]" is shaped like a C. The blocks in it make the sound of one track.
  • The blocks in it read from top to bottom. The top block makes the sound (s, note, seq and others), and the blocks under it change it (fast, effects and others), in the same order as s("hh").fast(8) in Strudel.
  • Round blocks are values, which go where a number or text goes.

Three levels of blocks

Only a few blocks are shown at first. Show more blocks as you get used to them.

Choose "normal" or "master" with ▼ of the top block of "Strudel" and click the block. When you open a project, the level changes to the one the project uses.

LevelWhat you can doSteps
BeginnerChoose sounds with ▼, put them in order and layer them. Move sprites with the music1〜7
NormalWrite songs with mini-notation, lists, rhythms, effects, scales and your own voice8〜11
MasterTricks of club music: acid, trance gate, pumping and more. Techniques which make four on the floor cool12〜14
